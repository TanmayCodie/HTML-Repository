Good Health

Good health relies on a combination of balanced daily habits, mental well-being, and preventive care.
Core Pillars of Health-
• Nutrition: Focus on whole, natural foods like fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds, whole grains, and lean proteins. Limit sugary drinks, ultra-processed foods, and added sugars.
• Physical Activity: Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity cardio each week, alongside strength-training exercises twice a week.
• Sleep: Get 7 to 9 hours of continuous, quality sleep each night to support metabolism, brain function, and physical recovery.
• Stress Management: Reduce chronic stress using techniques like meditation, breathing exercises, spending time outdoors, or engaging in hobbies.
